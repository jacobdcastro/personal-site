## Background

My education has been mostly self-directed via books and online courses, with
the exception of my first community college CS101 class in 2015. I took a few
years off from tech for personal reasons, and returned in 2018 to continue my
code learning journey.

Since then, I've joined multiple early-stage startups in founding engineer
roles, including a YCombinator startup, where I learned the ins and outs of the
Bay Area tech grind and even spent a short stint living in a founder house,
where I fell in love with startups.

## Prior Work

This led me to take my first stab at becoming a founder myself, building a
product that I found many companies needing at the time. I spent many months
building the product with my co-founder, talking to users, and speaking with
investors to raise a pre-seed.[^neovim]

Eventually, I burned through my personal runway and ultimately sunsetted the
project.

## Current Work

I'm now a Senior Full Stack Engineer at Phylax Systems [[1]](#ref-1), working
in the crypto decentralized finance (DeFi) security space and building a
blockchain-based financial security product to protect user funds. I've had the
privilege of growing more here than any other company, raising my code quality
standards and security best practices; raising the bar in all the ways a
security product calls for.

On the side, I'm tinkering with some personalized custom AI workflows
[[2]](#ref-2) to help me ship better code quicker. I also love to read
technical books, currently: Designing Data-Intensive Applications
[[3]](#ref-3).

## Outside of Work

I enjoy both watching and playing sports, working out, traveling, reading books
and spending time with family. Check out my now page [[4]](#ref-4) to see what
I've been up to more recently.
